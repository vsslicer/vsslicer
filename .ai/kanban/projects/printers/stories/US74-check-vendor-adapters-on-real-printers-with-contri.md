As a contributor with one of these printers, I want a checklist per vendor and a recorded terms-of-service review, so that hardware reports can back the fixtures (no vendor hardware, D5).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A checklist per vendor lists what to verify on the printer and how to attach a recorded session as a fixture
- [ ] Each reverse-engineered adapter has a ToS review note that must be signed off before its extension release

## Out of scope

- Running the checklists.
