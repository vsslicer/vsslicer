As a owner of several printers, I want a Fleet view with each printer's state, groups and queued items, so that I see at a glance what is idle, what is printing and what is waiting.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Fleet tree with each printer's state and its queued items; cancel, retry and reorder from the view
- [ ] Groups of printers in settings, usable as a queue target
- [ ] A camera thumbnail per printer when the camera extension provides one

## Out of scope

- Cloud dashboards.
- Printer administration (the Printers view).
