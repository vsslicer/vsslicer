A maintainer gets every PrusaSlicer 2.9 and 3.0 key and every SuperSlicer key mapped to a canonical name with its native name and conversion, or marked native-only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every key of Prusa@b028299 and @30ef591 and SuperSlicer@1f3d287 is mapped or `native_only` with its dialect
- [ ] Prusa 3.0 YAML keys are tagged as pinned to `@30ef591`; legacy-only keys are flagged

## Out of scope

- Importing Prusa files (E12).
