status: reviewed

---

As the H2C owner, I want a real multi-spool print on the H2C to book `used_g` to the AMS spools I assigned, so that the Bambu path is proven on hardware.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A real H2C print with AMS spools assigned books `used_g` to the right spools and stores `remain` per tray (firmware version and date recorded)
- [ ] Whether the H2C reports `remain_g` is recorded
- [ ] Whether `used_g` includes flush and purge is recorded from scale readings before and after

## Out of scope

- Other printers on hardware
