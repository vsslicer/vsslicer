title: "Low spools warn me"
status: reviewed
epic: VSS:E42
---

As a user, I want a notification when a booked spool falls below a threshold, so that I change it before a print fails.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A spool booked below the configured threshold raises one notification; it does not repeat until the spool rises above the threshold

## Out of scope

- Reorder lists, cost reports
