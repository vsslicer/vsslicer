As a user, I want to export my inventory in Spoolman's `/export` shape, back it up and restore it, so that I can move or recover it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Export then import into an empty store reproduces the inventory
- [ ] Restoring a backup replaces the store and keeps the previous one as a backup

## Out of scope

- Spoolman sync (E45)
