As a user with locked plates, I want auto-orient to leave them alone and to offer arrange afterwards, so that my finished plates stay as they are.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Objects on locked plates never change under auto-orient, also under random command sequences
- [ ] The summary names skipped objects
- [ ] Accepting the offer runs the existing arrange command
- [ ] Verified only on fixtures (D5)

## Out of scope

- Arrange itself (existing)
