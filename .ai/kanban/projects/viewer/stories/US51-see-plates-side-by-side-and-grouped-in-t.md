title: "See plates side by side and grouped in the tree"
status: reviewed
epic: VSS:E34

As a user, I want plates drawn side by side like in Orca with the active plate highlighted, and the tree grouped by plate, so that I can see the whole job.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Plates are drawn side by side with the active plate highlighted
- [ ] The tree groups objects by plate and offers plate commands in its context menu
- [ ] Dragging an object between plates in the tree or the 3D view moves its instance
- [ ] Verified only on fixtures (D5)

## Out of scope

- Sending a plate to a printer (PRN)
