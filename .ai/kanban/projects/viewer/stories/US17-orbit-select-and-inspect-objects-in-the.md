As a user, I want to orbit, zoom, pick and hover objects and toggle wireframe, with the printer bed drawn, so that I can look around a model and tell what is where.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Orbit, pan, zoom, fit-to-view and a view cube work
- [ ] Hover and click picking uses three-mesh-bvh and highlights the object
- [ ] Wireframe toggle works; the bed is drawn from the project's printer bed shape when known
- [ ] Verified only on fixtures (D5)

## Out of scope

- Measure (E55)
