As a user, I want connectors on the cut face so that the printed parts fit together again.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Plug, dowel and snap connectors (prism or frustum; triangle, square, hexagon, circle) are placed by clicking the cut face with size, depth and clearance
- [ ] Dovetail cuts with angle, depth and count
- [ ] Generated plug and hole pairs differ only by the configured clearance, measured on fixtures
- [ ] Both parts remain closed manifold after connectors
- [ ] Verified only on fixtures (D5)

## Out of scope

- Cut plane placement (US87)
