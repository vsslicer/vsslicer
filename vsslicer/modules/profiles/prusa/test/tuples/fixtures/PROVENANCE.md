# Provenance: tuples

Fixtures for `test/tuples/tuples.test.ts` (PRF-42). Expectations live in `test/tuples/oracle.ts`.

- `PrusaResearch-cut.ini`: copied verbatim from `resources/profiles/PrusaResearch.ini` of
  PrusaSlicer@b028299c770b8380ee81c921a2867d522f288123 (tag `version_2.9.6`, AGPL-3.0). It keeps, in upstream order,
  the `[vendor]` section, the `[printer_model:*]` sections of the 11 models the tuples use, and every `[printer:*]`,
  `[print:*]` and `[filament:*]` section in the `inherits` closure of the 17 Prusa tuples (132 sections). The comment
  lines between sections and the file's `# Prusa Research` header line are dropped; nothing else is changed. The line
  numbers cited in `oracle.ts` are those of the upstream file. Upstream data, AGPL-3.0.
- `superslicer.ini`: written for this repository in SuperSlicer's INI dialect (the SuperSlicer@1f3d287 clone, AGPL-3.0,
  ships no vendor bundle). The first line is the `# generated by SuperSlicer` header `src/libslic3r/Config.cpp:2080`
  writes; `z_step`, `thumbnails_color`, `perimeter_round_corners`, `gap_fill_last`, `bridge_type`, `infill_dense`,
  `top_fan_speed`, `external_perimeter_fan_speed` and `filament_max_speed` are SuperSlicer-only keys. The sections are
  laid out so that each tuple's parents disagree, which is what makes the last-parent-wins rule observable.
  AGPL-3.0-or-later.
