As a user, I want a layer range slider, a move slider, feature and travel toggles, a legend, and a side panel with thumbnails, object labels (hover highlights the object), summary and plate picker, so I can inspect any part of a print.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Scrubbing the move slider updates only the visibility range, asserted by counting buffer uploads
- [ ] Layer range, toggles, colour mode, legend and top/iso/front views work on the corpus
- [ ] Side panel shows thumbnails, labels with hover highlight, summary with slicer estimate next to ours, and a plate picker for `.gcode.3mf`
- [ ] The H2C `.gcode.3mf` fixture opens by plate
