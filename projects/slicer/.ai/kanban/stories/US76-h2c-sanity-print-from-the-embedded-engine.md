As the owner of an H2C, I want a fixture sliced by the embedded engine printed once, so that its output is sanity-checked on hardware.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One fixture sliced by the embedded engine prints on the H2C if the engine has an H2C profile; firmware recorded
- [ ] Otherwise the check is recorded as skipped with the reason
