As a maintainer, I want backup and restore of the database and storage, and a published notice-and-takedown procedure with a contact, so that the registry survives a lost host and meets the licensing policy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Backup then restore into an empty instance serves the same extensions and versions (rehearsal recorded)
- [ ] The takedown procedure and contact are published on the registry's about page
- [ ] The registry policy is written down: licence rules, source link for copyleft extensions, namespace policy

## Out of scope

- Moderation staffing; legal review (no counsel, D5).
